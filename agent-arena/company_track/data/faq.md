# FAQ

**Do you take reservations?** No, walk-in only. Groups of 8+ can call ahead.

**Is the broth vegan?** Only the Stratus Veggie Ramen broth (miso). Tonkotsu is pork based.

**Can I change my delivery address after ordering?** Within 5 minutes of placing the order, in the app.

**Do you have Wi-Fi?** Yes. Ask the cashier for today's password.

**Are you hiring?** Yes! Apply at careers.nimbusnoodle.example.
