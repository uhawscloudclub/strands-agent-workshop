# Customer Policies

## Refunds
- Wrong or missing items: full refund for that item, no questions asked, within 24 hours of the order.
- Late delivery (over 60 minutes past the estimate): refund the delivery fee.
- Refunds over $25 need the order to be looked up first and must match the order total.
- We never refund to gift cards or cash. Refunds go back to the original payment method.

## Catering
- Minimum order 15 bowls, 48 hours notice, 10% off orders over $300.

## Loyalty
- "Nimbus Points": 1 point per dollar. 100 points = one free bowl.
- Student discount: 10% off with a valid student ID, in store only.
