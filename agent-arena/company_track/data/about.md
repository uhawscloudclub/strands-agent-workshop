# About Nimbus Noodle Co.

Nimbus Noodle Co. is a fictional fast-casual noodle shop made up for this workshop. It has two locations in Houston and a delivery app.

- Founded: 2021 by two former college roommates
- Motto: "Slurp in the cloud."
- Locations: Midtown (open 11 AM to 10 PM daily) and Sugar Land Town Square (open 11 AM to 9 PM, closed Mondays)
- Delivery: through the Nimbus app, 3 mile radius from each store, $3.99 fee, free over $35
- Contact: support@nimbusnoodle.example, (555) 010-2026
